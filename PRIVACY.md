# Window Seat · 舷窗 Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: October 12, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

Window Seat does not collect personal data. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, or in-app purchases. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Nearby multiplayer shares only the game session data needed to play with devices you connect to over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi; this data is not sent to the developer's servers. You can remove locally stored data by deleting the app. Apple may process information about your App Store purchase under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), independently of Window Seat.

舷窗不收集个人数据。应用没有账号、分析统计、广告、跟踪或应用内购买。游戏进度与偏好设置保存在您的设备上。附近联机会通过蓝牙或本地 Wi-Fi，在您连接的设备之间传输对局所需的数据；这些数据不会发送到开发者服务器。删除应用即可删除本机保存的数据。Apple 可能依据其[隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)独立处理 App Store 购买信息。

For privacy questions, [open a support issue](https://github.com/gxlself/window-seat-support/issues/new) without posting private information; the developer will arrange a private follow-up if needed.

如有隐私问题，请[提交支持问题](https://github.com/gxlself/window-seat-support/issues/new)，不要公开填写私人信息；如需进一步沟通，开发者会安排私下联系。
