Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

你的游戏时间，
只属于你。

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026 年 10 月 24 日

简要说明

舷窗不收集个人数据。没有账号、分析统计、广告、跟踪或应用内购买。游戏进度和偏好设置保存在你的设备上。

数据与设备

游戏进度和设置储存在设备本机。附近多人游戏通过蓝牙或本地 Wi-Fi 在你连接的设备之间传输对局所需的数据；这些数据不发送到开发者服务器。应用不要求登录。删除应用可删除本机保存的数据。

Apple 的服务

购买和下载由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。Apple 可能依照其隐私政策独立处理购买信息，这不属于舷窗开发者收集的数据。

Android 版

Android 版从 GitHub 下载，并会检查更新：开启「自动检查更新」（默认开启）且已联网时，最多每 24 小时向 GitHub 查询一次新版本；也可在设置里手动检查。点「立即更新」后才会从 GitHub 下载安装包。这些请求不包含账号、设备标识或游戏数据，GitHub 会看到你的 IP 地址、请求时间和应用版本号，并依照其隐私声明处理。自动检查可在设置中关闭。

联系与更新

如对隐私有疑问，请发送邮件至 gxlself@gmail.com。政策若有变化，将在此页面更新生效日期。

English

Window Seat does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking, or in-app purchases. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Nearby multiplayer shares only game session data with devices you connect to through Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi; it is not sent to developer servers. Delete the app to remove locally stored data. Apple may independently process App Store purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy. The Android version is downloaded from GitHub and checks for updates: when “Check for updates automatically” is on (the default) and a network is available, it asks GitHub for new releases at most once every 24 hours, and you can also check manually in Settings. The installer is downloaded from GitHub only after you tap Update. These requests carry no account, device identifier, or game data; GitHub sees your IP address, the request time, and the app version, and processes them under GitHub's Privacy Statement. You can turn off automatic checks in Settings. For privacy questions, email gxlself@gmail.com.
